Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Photo Sensei is built so that your photos and your choices stay on your iPhone. This page explains exactly what the app uses, and why.

No data collected

The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

No account

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Photos stay on your iPhone

We never receive your photos, their details or your decisions.

Only Apple services

Apple handles the in-app purchase and looks up place names.

Who we are

Photo Sensei is an iPhone app published by OptiApp. For any question about this policy, write to info@optiapp.it.

Access to your photos

Photo Sensei asks for access to your photo library to show you your photos one by one and to carry out what you choose: add to Favorites or to an album, remove from an album, hide, or move to Recently Deleted.

Nothing changes in the Photos app until you confirm the session recap. You can allow full or limited access, and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves a few things on your device only, and never sends them to us:

  • Which photos you have already sorted, and the choices you haven't confirmed yet, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your settings: what each swipe does, the language, moments and their length.
  • How many photos you have sorted for free and whether you have unlocked the app. This is kept in the iOS Keychain, so reinstalling the app doesn't reset the free photos.

Deleting the app deletes everything above except the Keychain entry, which iOS keeps on the device.

What goes through Apple

  • In-app purchase. The unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Place names. To show where a photo was taken, its coordinates are sent to Apple's location service, the same one the Photos app uses. Only the coordinates are sent, and only to Apple.
  • Ratings. The request to rate the app is shown by iOS.
  • Crash reports. Only if you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain no photos and nothing that identifies you.

What we never do

  • We have no account system, no server and no database of users.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party service or SDK that collects data. The app is built only with open-source libraries that work on your iPhone, plus Apple's own frameworks.
  • We never receive, sell or share your photos, their location or any other personal data.

Exporting albums

When you export albums as a .zip, the file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share it, for example AirDrop, Mail or Files.

Children

Photo Sensei collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your personal data, there is nothing we hold that we could show, correct or delete for you. You can remove what the app saved by deleting the app.

If you write to us, we use your email address only to reply to you, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
