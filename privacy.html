Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Photo Sensei does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The app works entirely on your iPhone. It reads your photo library only to show you your photos and to carry out what you choose (add to Favorites, add to an album, move to Recently Deleted). Your photos and the decisions you make are never sent anywhere.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. It does not send anything to us. Only two things go through Apple: the in-app purchase is handled by the App Store, and to show the name of the place where a photo was taken, its coordinates are sent to Apple’s location service (the same one the Photos app uses).

When you export an album, the .zip file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to share it.

The list of photos you have already sorted and your swipe settings are saved only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
